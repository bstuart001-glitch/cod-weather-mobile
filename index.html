How to use

This app is a mobile wrapper for the official COD NEXLAB forecast viewer. Start with Open Forecast Home, select your model/product in COD, then use COD’s Share Page feature to copy the exact URL. Paste and save it under Paste COD Link.

For the best iPhone view, rotate sideways, then tap Hide Menu. Use Open Full Tab if COD blocks iframe controls on your device.

Weather imagery and model pages are from College of DuPage NEXLAB. This PWA does not scrape, cache, bulk download, or redistribute COD imagery.
